# 🤖 Avatar Chat - Quick Start

## What Is It?

An interactive animated avatar that asks caring questions and responds to your thoughts. It's like having a supportive friend who listens and helps you reflect.

## How to Access

1. Go to http://localhost:5174
2. Click **"Avatar Chat"** in the navigation (🤖 icon)
3. Click **"Start Conversation"**
4. Start chatting!

## What Happens

1. **Avatar greets you** with a personalized question based on your mental health concern
2. **You type your response** in the text area
3. **Avatar responds** with support and asks a follow-up question
4. **Conversation continues** as long as you want
5. **Click "End Conversation"** when done

## Example Conversation

```
Avatar: "Hi Sarah! 👋 I'm here to support you. How have you been sleeping lately?"

You: "Not great, I've been having trouble falling asleep"

Avatar: "I hear you. That sounds really difficult. You're not alone. 
What usually helps you relax before bed?"

You: "I try to read but my mind keeps racing"

Avatar: "That's a really healthy approach. I'm proud of you for trying. 
Have you tried any grounding techniques like deep breathing?"
```

## Features

✅ **Animated Avatar** - Cute character with blinking eyes and moving mouth
✅ **Smart Questions** - Tailored to your mental health concern
✅ **Supportive Responses** - Caring, non-judgmental replies
✅ **Typewriter Effect** - Messages appear naturally
✅ **Chat History** - See full conversation
✅ **Easy to Use** - Just type and press Enter

## Avatar Personality

- **Caring**: Listens without judgment
- **Supportive**: Validates your feelings
- **Curious**: Asks follow-up questions
- **Encouraging**: Celebrates your efforts
- **Understanding**: Acknowledges difficulties

## Tips

💡 **Be Honest** - Share your real thoughts and feelings
💡 **Take Your Time** - No rush, chat as long as you need
💡 **Reflect** - Use this for self-awareness
💡 **Combine Features** - Use with check-ins and assessments
💡 **Seek Help** - For crisis, use Crisis Resources page

## What the Avatar Asks

**If you have Depression:**
- How have you been sleeping?
- What brought you joy today?
- Are you taking care of yourself?
- How are your energy levels?
- Have you reached out to anyone?

**If you have Anxiety:**
- What's been worrying you?
- Have you tried grounding techniques?
- How's your breathing?
- What usually helps calm you?
- Are you feeling overwhelmed?

**If you have PTSD:**
- How are you feeling right now?
- Have you had any triggers?
- What's a safe place for you?
- How's your support system?
- What helps you feel safe?

**For General Wellness:**
- How are you feeling today?
- What's been on your mind?
- Have you done anything for yourself?
- What's one positive thing that happened?
- How can I support you?

## Important

⚠️ **This is a supportive tool, not professional help**
- For crisis situations, use Crisis Resources
- Combine with real human support
- Seek professional help when needed
- Conversations are not stored (unless backend is added)
